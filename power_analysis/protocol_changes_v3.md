## Decisions taken where the brief was silent or internally constrained (to confirm)

1. **PAS in session-1 blocks.** Kept on no-probe trials as classifier labels and for the PAS-report version of C1a (the two-version rule requires it); it feeds no staircase. The literal reading of "PAS only in the titration phase" would remove both. Alternative if the authors insist on the literal reading: train the classifier on an enlarged session-1 titration phase (about 200 rated trials, +20 min) and drop the PAS-report version of C1a — the two-version rule would then have to be redefined.
2. **Titration phase length** 48 trials at 8 s (6.4 min), a design choice to be replaced by the pilot's staircase length; the session-2 duration of 104.6 min depends on it (98.2 min without it).
3. **Usable-trial floor as an exclusion** now applies to the three C1a analysis cells only; count cells below the floor get the repeated-block provision and, if still short, the participant is retained for C1a and flagged in the count estimate. Without this change, the count cells (P = 0.6644 jointly) would reintroduce the floor risk the third block removes.
4. **Rating-scale citation.** The brief cites the scale as "Morsella et al. 2009 (Exp Brain Res 205)". The 8-point urge-to-err scale and the t(13) = 6.21 anchor are from Morsella, Gray, Krieger & Bargh, *Emotion* 2009, 9, 717–728 (S5 ref [15]; the *Experimental Brain Research* item is the 2013 fMRI follow-up, which cites it). S5 cites [15]; §9.1 cites [40], which the current §9.1 already uses for "the theory's stated outcome is experienced conflict" and which is assumed to be the same paper in the manuscript list — to be verified against the reference list.
5. **Session-1 practice trials** (4 per block, 64 in all) are now counted in the session-1 duration; v2 did not count them.
6. **Block-order adjacency.** In 4 of the 14 sequences the third block of a C1a cell follows its second directly; disclosed in S5.6 rather than avoided, because avoiding it would break the Latin-square balance.
7. **Multiplicity across C1a and CR.** Treated as two families (different hypotheses, different outcome variables); both p-values reported so a reader can apply a joint correction.
